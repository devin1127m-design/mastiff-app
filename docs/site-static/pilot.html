The pilot

Free for thirty days. On day 30, you decide.

For offices that pay vendors by wire, ACH or check. No payment card required, and nothing renews automatically. If you want to continue, we discuss terms on day 30. If not, you keep the vendor file and the log.

Mastiff is a new service. The pilot is limited to five offices, and terms may change once those places are filled.

What we do for you.

Five services, all included and all performed by Mastiff. Your bookkeeper's process stays the same.

  1. 01 The check

    Before it is keyed.

    Every payment request is reviewed by Mastiff before it is keyed. You receive Pass or Hold, with the reason stated in one line.

  2. 02 We make the calls

    We contact the vendor.

    On every hold, we call the vendor at the number already on file and report the result within one hour, Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Your designated second approver signs off. Your bookkeeper is never required to make the call.

  3. 03 The verified vendor file

    A vendor list you own.

    Every call is recorded against the vendor. By day 30, your office holds a vendor file in which each active vendor's phone number and account have been confirmed by voice, dated, and attributed to the person who confirmed them.

  4. 04 New vendor onboarding

    Before the first payment.

    For each new subcontractor or supplier, we collect the W-9, match the legal name, and confirm the account with the bank. Your office records only the last four digits.

  5. 05 If a payment goes wrong

    The first 24 hours, on one page.

    A checklist covering the bank recall request, FBI IC3 report and notice to your insurer, along with the log to provide them. We do not guarantee recovery.

On day 30, you receive a renewal packet: your payment record mapped to the questions insurance carriers ask, ready to share with your broker.

Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

What the thirty days look like.

  1. 01 Setup, day one

    Load the vendor list, set the limits.

    Thirty minutes with your bookkeeper to record each vendor's name, the last four digits of the bank account and the callback number already in use. The owner then sets the amount range, payment hours and who may act as second approver.

    Nothing to install. No access to your bank or accounting system.

  2. 02 The desk, day one onward

    Every payment request is checked before it is keyed.

    Your bookkeeper sends each request to Mastiff, which returns Pass or Hold with the reason. On a hold, we place the call and report the result. A hold is cleared by a logged callback to the number on file and a second approver.

  3. 03 The review, weekly

    We go through the log with you.

    Each week we review what was held, released or rejected, how long holds took, and which vendors are now voice-confirmed. On day 30, you receive the renewal packet.

    No contract. You may stop at any time with one message.

Start with your past payments, at no cost.

Send us 18 months of payment history. We will show you what Mastiff would have held, and any payments that appear to have been made twice, so you can ask those vendors for a credit or refund. Include only the last four digits of account numbers, never full numbers.

Before year-end, we also prepare a 1099 readiness list: which vendors are over the reporting line, which W-9s are missing, and what to collect before the filing deadline. Your accountant confirms every form.

For contractors, we also check each subcontractor's insurance certificates and lien waivers against what you have paid, so a lapsed policy or a missing waiver is flagged before the next payment rather than at your insurance audit.

About

A small service for small offices.

Mastiff is run by Devin Martin from Oxford, Michigan. Devin has managed the finances for a construction company, so he knows how payments go out in a small office. You deal with the person who built it and who makes your calls. There is no sales team and no call center.

Why small offices.

Large companies buy payment-verification software sold by quote. A small office usually has one bookkeeper, a checkbook and a bank login, and a request that looks right can be paid before anyone asks a second question. Mastiff asks that question, makes the call, documents the answer and leaves the decision with you.

What Mastiff does for you.

  • We check each payment request before it is keyed and return Pass or Hold with the reason.
  • We make the calls on your behalf, to the number already on file, and report what was said.
  • We keep the verified vendor file: each vendor's phone and account, confirmed by voice, dated and attributed.
  • We onboard new vendors: the W-9, the name match and the bank confirmation before the first payment.
  • We hand over the incident playbook for the first 24 hours if a payment goes wrong. We do not guarantee recovery.

Your office still makes every payment decision, and a second person at your office signs off on each hold. Mastiff never moves money and never blocks a payment.

How we work.

  • We flag. We do not decide. Your firm makes every payment decision.
  • We make the verification calls on every hold, to the number already on file, never to a number in the request.
  • We hold only what the check supports, and we use plain language: "would have held," "worth checking," "missing."
  • We store the payee, the amount, the last four digits of an account (nothing longer) and the callback record: who called, the number, what was said and who approved. On request, we delete your files at the end of the pilot.
  • We state plainly what we have not yet done. Mastiff is a new service and has no client logos or testimonials to show.

Where we are.

Oxford, Michigan, serving offices in Oakland County.

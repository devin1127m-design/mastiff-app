Running the pilot

  • 1
    Load the vendor listName, bank, last four and the phone number you already have. Thirty minutes, once.
  • 2
    Every payment request gets checked firstInvoices, bank changes, wires. Pass means key it to the account on file. Hold means nobody pays it yet.
  • 3
    Holds end with a callbackSomeone other than the person who received the request calls the number on file, and logs the call here with a second person's name. Then it is released or rejected.
  • 4
    The log is yoursEvery request, every hold, every callback, with who did what and when. The paper your insurer asks for.

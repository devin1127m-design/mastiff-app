# R1: What your insurer requires (requirements matrix)

Status: MERGED from the three research files in this folder; no new research. Dated 2026-10-24 research. Not legal or coverage advice.

How to read
- Citations are "(file#carrier; document, date)". Files: **insurtech** = insurtech-carriers.md, **traditional** = traditional-carriers.md, **specialty** = specialty-brokers-caselaw.md. Quotes are as the inputs gave them.
- **[secondary]** = the input calls it secondary, page-fetch, search-snippet, third-party or unverified. Unmarked = input says primary text-extracted (or a court opinion read).
- **Type**: condition = coverage applies only if the step was done; exclusion = loss excluded unless done; application question = a representation on the application (misstatement can support rescission); none found = no verification wording in documents read.
- "not found" = the inputs have nothing. Sublimit and deductible figures are mostly from negotiated public-entity policies or marketing, not small-business norms.

## What the log must record

Designed to satisfy the strictest wording: Chubb MS-371960.2 (contemporaneous written record, non-email, before payment, two approvers incl. an Executive), Intact M350 (recorded/logged, produce documentation on claim), Beazley (method other than the original request), Coalition endorsement (different method; contact info not from the instruction), One80/PSG and Travelers questions (different caller, different person answering, hold until response). Short names: Chubb = MS-371960.2; Intact = M350; Coalition = CYUSP-50EN-000108-0225-01; PSG = One80/PSG Fraudulent Impersonation Questionnaire; Trav = CRI-14104 / CYB-14302 / CRI-N-14300.

| # | Field | Wording it satisfies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Request received: name of receiver, date/time, channel (email, phone, portal, text), and a copy of the request | Beazley "original means of request"; Coalition "original method of authentication"; Trav II.4a "individual who received the change request"; Chubb "Bank Account Request" |
| 2 | Request type: bank/wire change, new vendor, invoice payment, or internal-employee instruction; payee; amount | Chubb branches (A)/(B)/(C); Coalition and At-Bay $25,000 thresholds; Hartford I.4 (new bank, foreign); Trav II.5 recent change |
| 3 | Callback number used, plus its origin: source record and date it was on file before the request arrived | Coalition "contact information that is not obtained from the fraudulent instruction"; Trav II.4 / PSG C.4 "telephone number provided by the Vendor before the change request was received"; Beazley app "predetermined number"; Intact "pre-arranged callback" |
| 4 | Channel of verification, confirmed as different from the request channel and not email | Chubb "other than e-mail"; Coalition "different from the original method"; Beazley "method other than the original means of request"; Cowbell 5.2 "secondary method" |
| 5 | Caller: name of person who made the call, checked as different from the receiver (field 1) | Trav II.4a; PSG A.3 / B.6a / C.4a "employee other than the employee who receives" |
| 6 | Time of call (start time) and every attempt, including unanswered ones and voicemail | Chubb "attempted to verify... contemporaneously documented"; Intact "recorded, logged"; SJ Computers fact pattern (unanswered call, paid anyway) |
| 7 | Who answered: name and role at the vendor, and confirmation they are not the person who sent the request | Trav II.4c; PSG C.4b; SPG ExecuPro "someone at the vendor, other than the person who sent the request"; Trav guidance "speaking to a known voice" [secondary] |
| 8 | What they said: confirm, deny or changed details, written at the time | Chubb "result of such attempt in writing"; Intact "recorded, logged or otherwise documented" |
| 9 | Hold status: payment or change blocked until the vendor responds; release time later than call time | Trav II.4b / PSG B.6b "refrain from... until after the vendor has responded"; Chubb "prior transferring"; Intact "prior to transferring" |
| 10 | Second approver: name, role (flag Executive), time, different from initiator and receiver, ideally different device | Chubb (A) "at least two Employees, one of whom is an Executive"; Coalition "different computer and/or device"; Intact "second individual"; PSG A.1-A.2; Trav II.4d / IV.2; At-Bay "at least two employees" |
| 11 | Outcome: released, rejected, escalated, or changed; reason | Chubb/Intact result; Beazley "not first authenticated" (no release without it) |
| 12 | Documentation retained: call recording or notes, request copy, attachments, immutable timestamps, exportable per claim | Intact (f) "electronic recording or other documentation"; Chubb "in writing"; Beazley/Hiscox proof of loss (90 days Hiscox) |
| 13 | Optional: new-account checks (bank call, account age) and incident time for reporting | Trav II.1, II.5; PSG C.2; Coalition 72-hour reporting [secondary] |

## Broker and application questions (verbatim, deduplicated)

Each question is quoted from the first source listed; "~" = same question in near-identical words.

1. "Does the Applicant confirm all change requests regarding Vendor account information... by a direct call to the Vendor using only the telephone number provided by the Vendor before the change request was received?" Asked by Travelers (CRI-14104 II.4, CYB-14302 Q6, CRI-N-14300 Q5), One80/PSG (C.4), Intact 71074, SPG ExecuPro ~, Beazley F00769 ~ (predetermined number).
2. "Is the call back procedure performed by an individual other than the individual who received the change request?" Travelers II.4a; PSG A/B/C; SPG ExecuPro ~.
3. "Does the Applicant refrain from implementing any such change requests until after the Vendor has responded...?" Travelers II.4b; PSG ~; SPG ~.
4. "Does the Applicant confirm all such change requests made by a Vendor with an individual (at the Vendor) other than the individual who requested the change?" Travelers II.4c; PSG C.4b; SPG ExecuPro.
5. Supervisor approval of vendor change: "Does the Applicant require that all such change requests made by a Vendor be approved by the Applicant's supervisor(s)...?" Travelers II.4d; PSG C.4c; Intact "second individual"; Marsh AU "senior management approval".
6. "Prior to executing an electronic payment, do you verify the validity of the funds transfer request or payment change request, with the requestor, via a separate means of communication...?" Corvus Q19; Cowbell 5.2 ~; Hartford CyberChoice section 7 ~; Coalition 2020 ~ ("secondary means of communication").
7. "Do payments or funds-transfers of a certain amount require dual authorization?" (and "what is that amount?") Travelers IV.2 / CRI-N-14300 Q3; PSG A.2; Hartford HR 00 H122 I.4; SPG ExecuPro; Intact 71074; Coalition ("in excess of $25,000"); At-Bay ($25,000, two employees).
8. "Is the authority to make electronic funds transfers... limited by the amount of each transfer?" Travelers IV.4 (also IV.6-IV.7 per-person limits).
9. "Do you require all outgoing wire transfers be subject to segregation of duties between initiation and authorization...?" PSG A.1; Beazley ("Can the employee independently initiate a wire transfer?"); Cowbell 5.3 ~; Marsh AU reconciliation by persons not authorised.
10. Internal requests: "Are wire transfer instructions made from an internal source... verified by calling back the requestor at a pre-established telephone number...?" PSG A.3; Hartford I.2; SPG ExecuPro.
11. New vendor bank account: "Is all vendor bank information verified by a direct call to the receiving bank prior to the account being established...?" PSG C.2; Travelers II.1; Cowbell 5.1 ~; Intact 71074 (legitimacy of new vendors).
12. "Does the Applicant have procedures in place to verify the authenticity of invoices and other payment requests received from a Vendor?" Travelers II.2; PSG C.3; Hartford II.10; Beazley AP invoice questions.
13. "Does the Applicant have procedures in place to verify the receipt of inventory, supplies, goods or services against an invoice prior to making payment?" Travelers II.3 / Q4; Beazley (invoice vs purchase order and receiving report).
14. "Does the Applicant verify the length of time the account receiving the payment... has been in existence...?" Travelers II.5 only.
15. "Does the Applicant maintain a pre-established list of employees authorized to initiate payment or funds transfer requests...?" Travelers IV.1; Hartford I.1; Beazley ("Who in your organization has the authority to initiate a wire transfer?").
16. Anti-fraud training: "Are all employees who are responsible for authorizing and executing payments... provided anti-fraud training, including social engineering, phishing...?" Travelers I.2; Hartford I.5; PSG A.4; Beazley; At-Bay Q12 ~; Corvus Q18 ~.
17. Fraud history: "Has the Applicant received fraudulent emails within the last twelve months...?" Beazley (12 months); SPG ExecuPro (3 years); Hartford III.12 / PSG / Travelers CRI-N-14300 Q9 (3-year loss history); Intact 71074 (6 years).
18. Beazley only: "Does your organization have a documented written response plan for fraudulent instructions that includes instructions to contact the FBI/IC3...?"; "Are international and domestic funds transfer procedures performed consistently...?"
19. Other single-source: "Does the Applicant reconcile transferred funds on a daily basis?" (Hartford I.3; Marsh AU similar); "Are certain employees with authority to approve electronic funds transfers... required to be available at all times by cell phone?" (Travelers IV.5); "Are these procedures used without exception?" (Intact 71074); "Verification is required for all transfer instructions in excess of $____" (PSG rating section).
20. Instruction on forms: "Attach a separate sheet... with an explanation for any 'No' answers" (Travelers CRI-14104; PSG). Marsh AU: "Any unsatisfactory answers will lead to standard underwriting review."

## Cases

Inputs found no decision upholding a denial for a skipped callback or missing dual control (specialty#gaps; traditional#gaps). Cases below show sublimits and wording, not callback penalties.

- Mississippi Silicon Holdings v. Axis, No. 20-60215 (5th Cir. 4 Feb 2021): insured had a three-step approval process, wired about $1.025M, paid only the $100,000 SE limit; primary (opinion read).
- SJ Computers v. Travelers, No. 21-cv-2482 (D. Minn. 12 Aug 2022): CEO's call went unanswered, he paid $593,555 anyway; held SE not computer fraud, $100,000 limit only; primary (opinion read in traditional); secondary in specialty.
- Perry & Perry Builders v. Cowbell/Obsidian (W.D. Tex. 9 Mar 2026): two wires (~$875k) held one $250,000 loss, one limit owed; MSJ filed 7 Jul 2025 is primary, the ruling is known from law-firm blogs only, so secondary.
- Future Electronics v. Chubb Canada, 2020 QCCS 3042 (29 Sep 2020): about CAD 2.7M loss, $50,000 SEF sublimit paid, FTF/computer fraud held inapplicable; secondary (summary, opinion not read).
- Principle Solutions v. Ironshore, 944 F.3d 886 (11th Cir. 9 Dec 2019): $1.7M wire covered, "resulting directly" read as proximate cause, a confirming bank call did not break the chain; secondary.
- Ernst & Haas v. Hiscox (9th Cir. 2022): $200,000 email fraud, held for insured on both clauses; Hiscox crime form, not CyberClear; secondary.
- Gore, Kilpatrick & Dambrino v. Spinnaker, No. 4:25-cv-107 (N.D. Miss. 31 Mar 2026): dismissed because impersonated person was not an existing client; firm reportedly phoned before wiring, which did not rescue coverage; secondary.
- Office of the Special Deputy Receiver v. Hartford, No. 25-2309 (7th Cir. 18 Jun 2026): email-initiated transfer rider exclusion applied, dismissal affirmed; no callback facts; primary-derived (FindLaw) and secondary (Clausen Miller).
- Unnamed bank v. insurer (summary judgment 11 Feb 2020): two of three counts dismissed; insurer argued skipped callbacks but ruling on that point not stated; secondary (insurer counsel's case study).
- Lowenstein Sandler podcast ep. 71 (Aug 2023): an unnamed carrier reportedly denied a claim because the insured emailed the sender for a bank letter; no case named; secondary (traditional#cross-carrier).
- American Tooling Center v. Travelers (6th Cir. 2018): insured paid without verifying, held covered; known from summaries only, not read; secondary.

## What this means for the pitch

- Sublimits in the documents are $100,000 to $250,000 (Travelers $100,000, Chubb up to $250,000, Coalition $250,000, Cowbell $250,000) against losses of $268K to ~$1M, so preventing the payment is worth more than recovering it.
- The strictest wordings make verification part of coverage: Chubb MS-371960.2, Intact M350, Beazley's "not first authenticated" carve-out and Coalition's endorsement. Each needs a documented, non-original-channel check before payment; that record is the Mastiff log.
- Almost every questionnaire asks for the same four things: callback to a number on file before the request, caller different from receiver, answer from a different person at the vendor, and dual approval over a stated amount. The hold-until-logged design answers all four.
- Specimen and base forms for At-Bay, Corvus, Travelers CyberRisk, Hiscox CyberClear and Chubb have no verification condition, so for those carriers the callback is an application representation (rescission risk), not a coverage trigger; the log also proves the application answer was true.
- The outcome of cases here turned on limits and characterisation, not callbacks, and no denial for a skipped callback was found. Pitch the log as evidence for underwriting and for the endorsements that do condition cover, not as a guarantee of payment.

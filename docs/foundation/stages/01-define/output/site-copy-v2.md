### Three rules every office should have
Eyebrow: Three rules
Heading: Every case we studied came down to one of these.
Intro: The costume changes. The signs do not: money going to an account you have never paid, an urgent request outside the normal pattern, a skipped callback.
Rule 1 title: The callback rule.
Rule 1 text: Bank details change only after a call to a number already on file. Someone other than the person who received the request makes the call. The payment waits until the call is logged.
Rule 2 title: The cap.
Rule 2 text: Set a dollar amount above which no single person can send, and a weekly total. Write it down and enforce it at the bank with dual release.
Rule 3 title: The urgency rule.
Rule 3 text: A request to skip the process is a reason to slow down. The owner commits in writing never to be angry about a confirmation call.

### Introduction

### The three holds
Eyebrow: Holds
Heading: Three things hold a payment on their own.
Hold 1 title: Payee not on file.
Hold 1 text: The vendor is not on your list. Mastiff shows "Payee missing from your vendor list. Worth checking before payment."
Hold 2 title: Bank details changed.
Hold 2 text: The account differs from the last four digits on file. Mastiff shows "Bank details differ from the file. Would have held."
Hold 3 title: Amount out of range.
Hold 3 text: The amount is above the range set for that vendor or for your office. Mastiff shows "Amount is outside the usual range. Worth checking."

### The two cautions
Eyebrow: Cautions
Heading: Two signals add weight. They never hold a payment alone.
Caution 1 title: Off-hours.
Caution 1 text: The request arrived outside your payment hours, such as 9 PM on a Saturday.
Caution 2 title: Urgency language.
Caution 2 text: The request pushes you to hurry or to skip a step.
Combined rule: A caution holds a payment only when it appears together with one of the three holds. On its own, a caution is noted on the log and the payment passes.

### What happens at a hold
Eyebrow: Resolving a hold
Heading: A hold closes with a call and a second name.
Step 1: Your bookkeeper tells Mastiff about the hold, by phone, text or email. [CONFIRM: channels]
Step 2: Mastiff calls the vendor on the number already on file. Not a number from the email.
Step 3: Within the hour, Monday to Friday, 7 am to 7 pm, we send the result: who answered and what was said. The call is logged against the vendor.
Step 4: A second person at your office signs off. The firm decides to pay, change or reject the payment. The decision and both names are recorded.
New vendors: before the first payment we collect the W-9, match the name, and confirm the bank by a call to the bank. Your office records only the last four digits.
Note: If the call does not confirm the request, the payment is not made. The invoice is paid to the account on file.

### What Mastiff stores
Heading: Only what the check needs.
Body: Payee name, amount, the last four digits of an account, and who submitted and who resolved each request. Mastiff rejects anything longer than four digits. It has no access to your bank or your books. See the privacy terms. [Button: Read the privacy terms -> /legal]

Mastiff ("we", "us") is operated from Oxford, Michigan. Questions about this page go to [CONFIRM: contact email]. [LAWYER] confirm the legal entity name once the Michigan LLC is formed.

### Privacy: what we collect from the pilot form
Your name, firm, email address, phone number, the number of payments you make each month, and any note you choose to add. We use this only to contact you about the pilot and to follow up. We do not sell it. We do not share it for advertising.

### Privacy: what the service stores
For each payment request your office enters, we store:
- The payee name.
- The amount.
- The last four digits of the account number. Nothing longer.
- The date and time of the request.
- Who submitted the request and who resolved it.
- The result (pass or hold), the reason shown, and the callback log: who called, who answered, the outcome, and the name of the second person who signed off.
- Notes your staff choose to add.

### Privacy: what we never store
We never accept or store a full bank account number, routing number or tax ID. The service rejects any entry longer than four digits. We never ask for bank logins. Mastiff has no access to your bank accounts or your accounting books. Please do not type account numbers into the notes field or the pilot form.

### Privacy: how we use data
Only to run the checks, show your office its own log, support the pilot, and improve the service. Your office's data is visible only to the members of your office we have invited and to Mastiff staff when needed to support you. We do not use your payment records to advertise.

### Privacy: service providers
We use Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (website hosting) and Resend (email). They process data on our behalf. [LAWYER] confirm the data-processing terms and the list of providers before launch.

### Privacy: security
Each office can see only its own records. Access is by emailed sign-in link. We limit what we hold so that a breach would expose less. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise otherwise. [LAWYER]

### Privacy: how long we keep data

### Terms: what the service is
Mastiff is a verification and advisory service. We compare each request with the information your office gave us and return a result and a reason. We do not move money. We do not block a payment. We do not confirm that any payee or request is genuine. Your firm decides whether to pay, and your firm is responsible for that decision.

### Terms: what flags mean
A flag is a prompt to check. "Would have held," "worth checking" and "missing" mean that a request differs from what is on file. They are not findings about any person or business. Mastiff may pass a request that is later found to be improper, and may hold one that is genuine. [LAWYER]

### Terms: your responsibilities
Keep your vendor list accurate. Call the number already on file, not a number in the request. Do not enter full account numbers. Keep your sign-in links private. Decide each payment yourself.

# Pilot engagement and confidentiality agreement (DRAFT, not reviewed by counsel)

**This draft must be reviewed by a Michigan lawyer before anyone signs it.**

Mastiff is an advisory check. You enter a payment, new vendor or bank-change request; Mastiff compares it with the information you gave us and answers PASS or HOLD with a reason. PASS means only that no listed check flagged anything. Mastiff does not move money, approve or release payments, call vendors, or touch your bank or books. Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision; your staff make the callback to the number on file and give the second sign-off. We cannot promise to flag every problem, or that every flag is right. Mastiff is not an insurer, our fee does not depend on payment size, and we do not advise on insurance.

For each vendor we store the name, bank name, last four digits of the account and the callback number on file; we never accept a full account number or tax ID. For each check we log what was entered, the result, the callback, the second approver and the release, as your staff report them. We do not hear calls or confirm who answered. The log is your record: export it any time; entries are never edited, only corrected by new entries. On your written request we keep it for a dispute. Either of us may stop any day by telling the other; you then have 30 days to export, then we delete your records (unless you asked us to keep them) and confirm in writing. ## Terms of the pilot

- The pilot lasts 30 days from the date below. There is no fee. Nothing renews automatically.
- Either side may end it at any time with one message to the other.
- The office keeps its log. Mastiff never moves money or blocks a payment.
- Mastiff deletes the office's files on request and at the end of the pilot, unless the office asks us to keep them.
- Michigan law governs. Disputes are heard in Oakland County, Michigan. [LAWYER: L13]

## Our five promises

1. **We use your information only to run your checks.** We do not sell it, share it, or use it for another office or for marketing. We will not combine your vendor information with another office's unless you agree separately in writing.
2. **We keep as little as we can.** We take only the last four digits of any account or tax ID. If a file arrives with more, we delete it and ask for a copy with those numbers removed. We never ask for bank logins.
3. **Two people, named providers, United States storage.** Only the two Mastiff people named in the schedule can see your data. It is stored encrypted in the United States with the providers in the schedule. No other service, including any AI tool, gets your files unless the schedule lists it. We tell you of any provider change as soon as we learn of it.
4. **We tell you fast.** If we confirm that someone who should not have your data has seen it, we tell you within 72 hours of confirming it and say what we know. Whether and whom to notify stays your decision.
5. **You can take it with you, and we delete it.** Export your vendor list and log any time. When the pilot ends you have 30 days to take a copy; we then delete your records within 30 days and confirm in writing. We keep only our own agreement records and what the law requires or a dispute needs.

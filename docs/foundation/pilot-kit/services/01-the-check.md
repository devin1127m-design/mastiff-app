# 1. The check

## What it is
Before a payment is keyed, your bookkeeper types the request into the Mastiff page. It answers PASS or HOLD with a one-line reason.

## What we do
- Set up the page on your bookkeeper's machine and load your vendors.
- Agree three numbers with you: payment hours, a single-payment cap and a weekly total.
- Test it with your bookkeeper: a known vendor, a bank change and a new payee.
- If a request reaches us by text or email, we run the same check and reply.

## What you do
- Have your bookkeeper check every request before keying it.
- Decide the cap and the weekly total. They are your numbers.

## What you get
A PASS or HOLD for every request. Only three things hold: the payee is not on file, the bank details changed, or the amount is out of range. Off-hours and urgent wording are shown as "worth checking" notes. Each check is written to your log.

## What it is not
Not a block. Nothing is stopped and no money moves through Mastiff. A PASS is not approval to pay. Last 4 digits only, never a full account number.

Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

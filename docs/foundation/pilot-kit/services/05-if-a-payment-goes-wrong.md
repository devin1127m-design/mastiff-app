# 5. If a payment goes wrong

## What it is
A one-page checklist for the first 24 hours after a payment may have gone to the wrong account. You get it on day one.

## What we do
- Stay on the phone while you work through the first two steps.
- Send you the log export within 24 hours.
- Send a dated list of every callback on record.
- Keep our own call notes.

## What you do
- Call your bank first, as the checklist says, and write down times and names.
- Tell your broker in the time your policy sets. The checklist is not legal advice.

## What you get
A printed checklist and, if needed, the log export and callback list, ready to give your bank, your broker or your lawyer.

## What it is not
Not a promise that money will come back. We do not say what your policy covers or requires. Your bank, broker and lawyer decide what applies.

Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

# 3. The verified vendor file

## What it is
A list of your vendors in which each phone number and account (last 4 only) has been confirmed by voice. Each line shows the date and who confirmed it.

## What we do
- Update the vendor's line after every call we make.
- Record the number used and where it came from.
- Mark a vendor "not yet" until it has been called.
- Re-check a vendor when its details change, and at least once a year.
- Send you the updated file with the weekly review.

## What you do
- Tell us if a vendor's phone or bank changes.
- Keep the file. It belongs to you, including if you stop.

## What you get
By day 30, a dated vendor file you can print or export as a spreadsheet, showing how many active vendors have been confirmed by voice.

## What it is not
Not a clearance. A line shows that a call took place and what was said, nothing more. It does not mean a vendor is safe to pay. Last 4 digits only.

Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

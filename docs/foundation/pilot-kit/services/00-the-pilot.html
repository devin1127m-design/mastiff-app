The Mastiff pilot

What it is

A free 30-day pilot in which Mastiff does the payment checking work with you. No card, nothing renews, and you can stop any day with one message.

What we do

  • First, a look-back on past payments showing what would have held.
  • 1. The check: every request is checked before it is keyed.
  • 2. We make the calls: we call the vendor on each hold and report back within the hour, Monday to Friday, 7 am to 7 pm.
  • 3. The verified vendor file: each call is recorded against the vendor.
  • 4. New vendor onboarding: W-9 name match and bank confirmation before the first payment.
  • 5. If a payment goes wrong: a first-24-hours checklist, plus the log and callback list.

What you do

  • Name a second person who signs off each hold.
  • Check each request before it is keyed, and tell us about each hold.
  • Spend 30 minutes a week on a review with us.

What you get

On day 30 a one-page read-out, your verified vendor file, and your log set beside the questions carriers commonly ask. Take it to your broker, then decide: continue, stop, or stop and keep the file.

What it is not

Not a block on payments, not a way to move money, not advice on what your insurer requires. We keep the last 4 digits of an account or tax ID, never more.

Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

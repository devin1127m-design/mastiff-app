# Mastiff: how it works for you

## What this is
Mastiff checks a payment request before you key it. It answers PASS or HOLD, with a one-line reason. When it says HOLD, we make the call to the vendor for you. Mastiff never moves money and never blocks a payment. Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

## Before you key any payment
1. Open the Mastiff page.
2. Enter five fields: payee, amount, request type, last 4 digits, how it arrived.
3. Read the answer.

**PASS** means carry on. **HOLD** means tell us.

## When it says HOLD
1. Tell Mastiff by phone, text or email [CONFIRM: number and email]. Give the payee and the amount.
2. We call the vendor on the number already on file. Never a number from the request. We send you the result within the hour, Monday to Friday, 7 am to 7 pm.
3. Your second person signs off. It cannot be the person who entered the request.
4. Record four things, or ask us to record them: who called, which number, what the vendor said, the outcome.

You may make the call yourself if you prefer. Nothing is ever blocked.

## New vendor
Send us the W-9 and the first invoice. We match the name and call the bank. We send you the result before the first payment.

## Never type more than the last 4 digits
Do not enter a full account number or tax ID. Last 4 only.

2. We make the calls

What it is

When a request holds, we call the vendor for you. We use the number you already had on file, never the one in the request.

What we do

  • Call the number already on file and say who we are.
  • Ask about the specific change, and take the name and role of the person who answers.
  • Send you the result within the hour, Monday to Friday, 7 am to 7 pm.
  • If nobody answers, tell you so within the hour. The hold stays a hold.
  • Write down who called, which number, what the vendor said and the outcome.

What you do

  • Tell us about each hold.
  • Have your second person sign off. It cannot be the person who entered the request.

What you get

A written result for every hold, with the time of the call, the number used, the name of the person who answered and what they said. If a vendor says the request was not theirs, the entry reads "Record not paid".

What it is not

Not advice on whether to pay. We never say a payment is safe to release. You decide, and your second person signs off.

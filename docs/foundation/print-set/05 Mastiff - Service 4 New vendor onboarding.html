4. New vendor onboarding

What it is

Before you pay a new vendor for the first time, we check who they say they are. The office decides whether to pay.

What we do

  • Match the name on the W-9 to the name on the invoice.
  • Call the vendor on a number you already hold from another source.
  • Call the vendor's bank to confirm the account. If the bank will not confirm, we use the vendor's phone confirmation and a bank letter or voided check.
  • For subcontractors, ask for the insurance certificate and sworn statement, and note anything missing.

What you do

  • Ask the vendor for a W-9 and send it to us. Keep the original in your own files.
  • Ask us before the first payment.

What you get

A short note before the first payment: name matched or not, vendor confirmed or not, bank confirmed or not. We keep the last 4 digits, the date and who confirmed.

What it is not

Not an approval of the vendor. We never copy a tax ID or keep a full account number. Missing insurance papers are "worth checking" notes and never hold a payment on their own.

Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

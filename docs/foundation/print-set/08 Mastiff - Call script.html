Call script

Say it as written. Stop after the question and let them answer.

1. A contractor owner

Opening: "Hi, this is Devin Martin in Oxford. I help contractors check payments before they go out. Have you got two minutes?"

Ask: "When a sub sends new bank details, who calls them?"

Offer: "I run a free look-back on 18 months of your payments and show you what would have held. If it is useful, I run a free 30-day pilot where we make the verification calls for you."

Send: "Export your payments from QuickBooks: Reports > Transaction List by Vendor, last 18 months, CSV."

Close: "Can you send that this week?"

2. A CPA or bookkeeping firm that pays vendors for clients

Opening: "Hi, this is Devin Martin in Oxford. I work with firms that pay bills on behalf of clients. Have you got two minutes?"

Ask: "Do you pay vendors on behalf of clients?"

Offer: "I run a free look-back on 18 months of one client's payments and show you what would have held. Then a free 30-day pilot where we make the verification calls and keep the vendor file."

Send: "Export one client's payments from QuickBooks: Reports > Transaction List by Vendor, last 18 months, CSV."

Close: "Can you send that this week?"

3. A firm that does not pay vendors

Opening: "Hi, this is Devin Martin in Oxford. I help offices check payments before they go out. Have you got one minute?"

Ask: "Do you pay vendors on behalf of clients?" If no: "Who do you know that does? A contractor or a firm that handles clients' bills."

Offer: "I offer them a free look-back on 18 months of payments, then a free 30-day pilot where we make the verification calls."

Send: "Could you introduce me by email? I will keep it to two lines."

Close: "Can you send that introduction this week?"

If they ask

  • Price: "The pilot is free. We talk on day 30."
  • Insurance: "Ask your broker what your policy needs. We keep a dated record of every call."
  • Account numbers: "Last four digits only."

Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

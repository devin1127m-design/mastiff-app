# Setup questionnaire (one time)

Answers feed `_config/product.md` and `_config/conventions.md`.

1. Who is Mastif for, by name, and what should it call them?
2. What are the three things you most want it to handle for you this month?
3. What must it never do without asking? What may it do without asking?
4. Which folders on this machine may it read? Which may it write?
5. Should it keep notes about you across sessions? Anything it must never store?
6. Where will it run: this PC only, or others too? Always with you present, or unattended?
7. How should it talk: length, tone, when to push back?
8. How will you know after two weeks that it is earning its keep?

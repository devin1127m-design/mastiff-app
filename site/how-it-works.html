How it works

Three checks. Two cautions. One decision, made by you.

Your bookkeeper submits a payment request. Mastiff returns Pass or Hold with a one-line reason. Mastiff never moves money and never blocks a payment; it tells your office what to verify first.

Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

Holds

Three things hold a payment on their own.

  1. Payee not on file.The vendor is not on your list. Mastiff shows "Payee missing from your vendor list. Worth checking before payment."
  2. Bank details changed.The account differs from the last four digits on file. Mastiff shows "Bank details differ from the file. Would have held."
  3. Amount out of range.The amount is above the range set for that vendor or for your office. Mastiff shows "Amount is outside the usual range. Worth checking."

Cautions

Two signals add weight. They never hold a payment alone.

  • Off-hours.The request arrived outside your payment hours, such as 9:00 p.m. on a Saturday.
  • Urgency language.The request pushes you to hurry or to skip a step.

A caution holds a payment only in combination with one of the three holds. On its own, it is noted in the log and the payment passes.

Resolving a hold

A hold closes with a call and a second name.

  1. Your bookkeeper notifies Mastiff of the hold by phone, text or email.
  2. Mastiff calls the vendor at the number already on file, never a number provided in the request.
  3. Within one hour, Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., we report the result: who answered and what was confirmed. The call is logged against the vendor.
  4. A second person at your office signs off. The firm decides whether to pay, amend or reject the payment, and the decision and both names are recorded.

If the call does not confirm the change, the payment should not be released to the new account. The invoice is paid to the account on file.

New vendors: before the first payment, we collect the W-9, match the legal name and confirm the account with the bank. Your office records only the last four digits.

Only what the check needs.

Mastiff stores only the payee name, the amount, the last four digits of the account, and who submitted and resolved each request. Entries longer than four digits are rejected. Mastiff has no access to your bank or your accounting system.

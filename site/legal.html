Privacy and terms

Effective date: not yet published.

This page has not yet been reviewed by counsel.

Summary

Mastiff is a verification and advisory service. It does not move money or block payments on its own. Mastiff flags. Your firm makes every payment decision.

Privacy: who we are

Mastiff ("we", "us") is operated from Oxford, Michigan. Questions about this page go to us through the pilot form.

Privacy: what we collect from the pilot form

Your name, firm, email address, phone number, the number of payments you make each month, and any note you choose to add. We use this only to contact you about the pilot and to follow up. We do not sell it. We do not share it for advertising.

Privacy: what the service stores

For each payment request your office enters, we store:

  • The payee name.
  • The amount.
  • The last four digits of the account number. Nothing longer.
  • The date and time of the request.
  • Who submitted the request and who resolved it.
  • The result (pass or hold), the reason shown, and the callback record: who called, the number called, who answered, what was said, and the name of the person who approved.
  • Notes your staff choose to add.
  • Your verified vendor file: each vendor's name, the last four digits of their account, the callback number on file, and the date and name of who confirmed it.

Privacy: what we never store

We never accept or store a full bank account number, routing number or tax ID. The service rejects any entry longer than four digits. We never ask for bank logins. Mastiff has no access to your bank accounts or your accounting books. Please do not type account numbers into the notes field or the pilot form.

Privacy: how we use data

Only to run the checks, make the verification calls, prepare your office's reports, support the pilot, and improve the service. Your office's data is seen only by your office and by Mastiff when needed to do this work. We do not use your payment records to advertise.

Privacy: service providers

The website is hosted on Vercel. The pilot form is stored with Supabase and sends us an email through Resend. They process data on our behalf. During the pilot, Mastiff keeps your payment records, callback records and verified vendor file on your behalf. We do not have access to your bank or accounting system; we receive only what you send us, such as your payment export.

Privacy: security

We keep each office's files separate. We limit what we hold so that a breach would expose less. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise otherwise.

Privacy: how long we keep data

We keep your records while your office uses Mastiff and for a set period after it stops. After that we delete them. If you stop the pilot, you keep your vendor file and log: ask us and we will send them. At the end of the pilot, we delete your files on request.

Privacy: your choices

You may ask us at any time to export or delete your data. We will send an export in a common format and delete the records on request, except for what the law requires us to keep. Contact us through the pilot form.

Terms: what the service is

Mastiff is a verification and advisory service. We compare each request with the information your office gave us and return a result and a reason. On a hold, we call the vendor on the number your office already has on file and tell you what they said. For a new vendor, we collect the W-9, match the name and confirm the bank before the first payment. We keep the verified vendor file and give you an incident playbook for the first 24 hours if a payment goes wrong, without any promise of recovery. We do not move money. We do not block a payment. A completed call is a record of what was said, not a guarantee that a payee or request is genuine. Your firm decides whether to pay, your second person signs off, and your firm is responsible for that decision.

Terms: what flags mean

A flag is a prompt to check. "Would have held," "worth checking" and "missing" mean that a request differs from what is on file. They are not findings about any person or business. Mastiff may pass a request that is later found to be improper, and may hold one that is genuine.

Terms: your responsibilities

Keep your vendor list accurate and tell us when a vendor's details change. Send each payment request through Mastiff before it is keyed. Do not pay a held request until our result is back and your second person has signed off. Do not enter full account numbers. Decide each payment yourself.

Terms: the pilot

The pilot is free for thirty days. No card is taken. Nothing renews. Either side may stop at any time with one message. We may change pilot terms for later offices. Any continuation after day 30 requires a separate written agreement.

Terms: no warranty

The service is provided as is during the pilot. We do not promise that it will be uninterrupted, error-free or that it will flag every improper request.

Terms: limit of liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, our liability to you is limited to the fees you paid us, which for the pilot is none. We are not liable for lost funds, indirect or consequential loss, or a payment decision your firm makes.

Terms: governing law

Michigan law governs these terms. Disputes go to the courts located in Oakland County, Michigan.

Terms: changes

We may update this page. We will post the new date at the top and, for material changes, email active pilot offices.

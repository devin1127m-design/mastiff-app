# If a payment went to the wrong account: the first 24 hours

_Work down the list in order. Write the time and the name of each person you speak to._

## Now
1. **Owner or bookkeeper:** call your bank's fraud line now. Ask for a recall or reversal of the wire or ACH. Note the time and the name of the person you spoke to.
2. **Owner or bookkeeper:** if you know the receiving bank, call it and say a payment may have been sent in error. Note the time and the name.

## Same day
3. **Owner:** file a complaint at ic3.gov (the FBI's complaint form). Keep the complaint number.
4. **Owner:** notify your cyber or crime insurer, or your broker, in writing, within the notice window in your policy. [CONFIRM: your policy's notice window and who to write to]
5. **Bookkeeper:** keep everything. The request email with full headers, the Mastiff log export, and a note of who approved what. Do not delete or edit anything.
6. **Bookkeeper:** tell the real vendor, on the number already on file, that a payment request in their name was not theirs. [CONFIRM: wording with your lawyer]

## Before the next payment
7. **Owner:** do not pay that vendor again until their details have been re-verified by a call to the number on file and a second person has signed off.

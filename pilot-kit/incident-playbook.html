If a payment went to the wrong account: the first 24 hours

Work down the list in order. Write the time and the name of each person you speak to.

Now

  1. Owner or bookkeeper: call your bank's fraud line now. Ask for a recall or reversal of the wire or ACH. Note the time and the name of the person you spoke to.
  2. Owner or bookkeeper: if you know the receiving bank, call it and say a payment may have been sent in error. Note the time and the name.

Same day

  1. Owner: file a complaint at ic3.gov (the FBI's complaint form). Keep the complaint number.
  2. Owner: notify your cyber or crime insurer, or your broker, in writing, within the notice window in your policy. [CONFIRM: your policy's notice window and who to write to]
  3. Bookkeeper: keep everything. The request email with full headers, the Mastiff log export, and a note of who approved what. Do not delete or edit anything.
  4. Bookkeeper: tell the real vendor, on the number already on file, that a payment request in their name was not theirs. [CONFIRM: wording with your lawyer]

Before the next payment

  1. Owner: do not pay that vendor again until their details have been re-verified by a call to the number on file and a second person has signed off.

Call Devin: [CONFIRM: phone]. We send you the log export and a dated list of every callback on record. We cannot promise any money will come back.
